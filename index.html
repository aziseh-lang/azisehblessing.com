About Me

A MISSED FOLLOW-UP CAN COST A PATIENT THEIR TRUST. I MAKE SURE THAT NEVER HAPPENS.

I started in the ward. Managing patients, coordinating care teams, making quick decisions under pressure. Nursing gave me something that no course or certification alone can: the ability to see the whole picture while still catching the small details that matter.

That foundation led me into healthcare operations, where I realized that most medical practices do not struggle because of bad medicine. They struggle because of broken systems, poor communication, and scattered workflows.

Today, I work with healthcare founders, clinics, telehealth startups, and health organizations as their Medical Virtual Assistant and operations partner. I handle what drains your time so you can focus on what you do best: caring for patients and growing your practice.
